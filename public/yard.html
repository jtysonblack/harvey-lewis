The Format

What is a
Backyard Ultra?

4.1
Miles per loop
Every loop is exactly 4.167 miles — 6.7 kilometers. A trail circuit that starts and ends at the corral. You run it. Then you do it again.
:00
On the hour
The bell rings at the top of every hour. You have 60 minutes to complete the loop and return. Fail to make it back in time — the race is over for you.
1
Last person standing
There is no finish line. The race ends when only one runner completes a loop that no other runner can finish. That person wins. Usually, nobody wins.
The Athlete

The teacher
who runs
forever.

Harvey Lewis is a high school teacher from Cincinnati, Ohio. He coaches, grades papers, and shows up to class on Monday morning — sometimes days after running 450 miles through the Tennessee backwoods without sleeping.

He is vegan. He credits his plant-based diet for his recovery speed and his ability to stay on his feet for days at a time. Between loops at Big's Backyard Ultra, he eats pad Thai and muffins.

Harvey first came to world attention at the 2021 edition of Big's Backyard Ultra, setting a world record with 354 miles. In 2023, he obliterated it — 108 consecutive loops, 450 miles, 108 hours. Four and a half days of running. The teacher from Cincinnati ran further than any human ever had.

Before all of that: Gandhi's Salt March (241 miles across India), Selma to Montgomery, Team USA at four IAU 24-Hour World Championships, the Badwater 135. The running was always tied to something larger than the sport.
